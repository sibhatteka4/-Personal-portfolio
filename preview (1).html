About Me

Welcome to my professional portfolio. I am Sibhat Teka, an anesthesia professional at Dessie Comprehensive Specialized Hospital and a lecturer in the Department of Anesthesia, College of Medicine and Health Sciences, Wollo University, Ethiopia.

My work combines clinical practice, higher education, and the development of effective learning experiences for anesthesia students. I am committed to evidence-based practice, patient safety, professional development, and student-centered teaching.

My academic interests include regional anesthesia, acute and chronic pain management, perioperative care, instructional design, and the use of digital technologies in health sciences education.
